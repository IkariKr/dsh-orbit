## Explicitly carried-forward gaps (out of scope for this tag)

1. **Hub-minted QR landing page**: the pairing engine mints
   `/auth?token=<code>` URLs, but the registry serves no `/auth` route and
   enforces a global query-string fence (`query-not-allowed`). The hub-hosted
   scan-to-land UX is a product change and requires a new candidate under the
   SOP. Node-side pairing (node-origin fallback) is the working path.
2. **Wildcard node-route subdomains**: `n-<32hex>.dsh.ikarikore.top` hostnames
   are not publicly resolvable; the operator has deferred the Cloudflare
   wildcard hostname + Advanced Certificate Manager decision and uses the
   tailnet as the operator access channel. Node routes are functional wherever
   the hostname resolves.
3. **Version field**: `package.json` `version` intentionally remains
   `0.4.0-rc.2` per repository convention (unchanged at `v0.7.0-rc.1` as well).
