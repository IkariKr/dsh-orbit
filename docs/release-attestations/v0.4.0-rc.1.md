## 5. Scope Boundary & Known Limitations

- **Reverse-connected nodes**: Not in v0.4; remain v0.5 scope.
- **Replay cache**: RouteIngress nonce replay cache is process-local memory. Restart resets the cache; the bounded security boundary is signed proof plus the 30-second timestamp skew window and single-Hub assumption.
- **No silent failover**: Node failure fails closed and never redirects traffic to another node.
- **Routing prerequisites**: Wildcard DNS, wildcard TLS, authenticated outer gateway, and preserved `Host` headers are required.
